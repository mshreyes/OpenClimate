### 2. Authoritative Assessments, Open Textbooks & Simulators
The second tab of the dashboard catalogs foundational reference materials across three pillars:
- **Intergovernmental Assessments & Global Status Reports**: IPCC Sixth Assessment Reports (WG I, WG II, WG III, Synthesis & Special Reports), UNEP Emissions Gap Reports, WMO State of the Global Climate, U.S. Fifth National Climate Assessment (NCA5), Copernicus European State of the Climate (C3S), and the IEA Net Zero Roadmap.
- **Open-Access University Textbooks & Computational Monographs**: *The Climate Laboratory* (Brian E. J. Rose), *Introduction to Climate Science* (Andreas Schmittner), *Practical Meteorology* (Roland Stull), *Climate System Dynamics and Modelling* (Hugues Goosse), *Introduction to Physical Oceanography* (Robert H. Stewart), *Principles of Planetary Climate* companion code (Raymond T. Pierrehumbert), and *Atmospheric Chemistry* lecture archives (Daniel J. Jacob).
- **Interactive Simulators & Empirical Data Portals**: IPCC WGI Interactive Atlas, University of Chicago Online Climate Models (MODTRAN, RRTM, GEOCARB), En-ROADS & C-ROADS Global Policy Simulators, NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory (Mauna Loa Keeling Curve), and NASA GISS Surface Temperature Analysis (GISTEMP v4).

## Contributing & Reporting Broken Links

External universities and intergovernmental agencies periodically reorganize their web domains, archive older course iterations, or migrate URLs.

* **Broken Links**: If you encounter a `404` or outdated course link, please use the built-in **MIT OCW Internal Search** or **Web Search Fallback** buttons on the dashboard to access the resource immediately, and **open an Issue** on this GitHub repository so the URL in `data.js` can be updated.
* **New Resources**: Pull requests adding verified open-access climate courses, computational notebooks, or authoritative regional climate assessments are welcome.
